Driving Licence Photo Requirements

The Ministry of Road Transport and Highways specifies these photo requirements for driving licence applications on the Sarathi and Parivahan portals:

  • Colour photo on a white or light background
  • Front-facing view with both eyes open and visible
  • Neutral expression — no smiling or open mouth
  • No sunglasses or tinted glasses — prescription glasses are acceptable if no glare
  • No headwear unless for religious reasons
  • Recent photo — should reflect your current appearance

How to Take a Good Driving Licence Photo at Home

Getting a professional-looking driving licence photo at home is easier than you think. Follow these practical tips to ensure your photo meets Sarathi/Parivahan requirements without needing to visit a studio:

  • Use a plain white wall as your background — make sure there are no patterns, textures, or shadows on the wall behind you.
  • Do not wear hats, caps, or headbands — the RTO requires a clear view of your head and hairline unless you wear headgear for religious reasons.
  • Avoid red-eye reduction features on your camera or phone — these can alter natural eye colour and cause rejection.
  • Both ears should be visible — position yourself so your face is squarely facing the camera for a proper passport-style shot.
  • Keep hair off your face — ensure your forehead, eyebrows, and both eyes are completely unobstructed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the driving licence photo size in mm?The Indian driving licence photo size is 35 mm × 45 mm (width × height) at 300 DPI, equivalent to 420 × 525 pixels.
What is the maximum file size for DL photo upload?The Sarathi/Parivahan portal accepts photos up to 50 KB in JPEG format.
Can I use the same photo for DL and Voter ID?While both are 35×45 mm, the DPI and file size requirements differ. Always check the specific requirements of each application.
Do I need a white background for driving licence photo?Yes, a plain white or off-white background is required for driving licence photos in India.
Is the DL photo size same for learner's licence and permanent licence?Yes, both the learner's licence (LL) and permanent driving licence require the same 35×45 mm photo at 300 DPI, under 50 KB. The photo specifications are identical across all RTOs in India.
Can I wear prescription glasses in my driving licence photo?Prescription glasses are allowed, but make sure there is no glare on the lenses and your eyes are clearly visible. Sunglasses or tinted glasses are not permitted.
What if I apply for a driving licence from a different state?The photo size requirements are uniform across all Indian states because the Sarathi and Parivahan portals follow Ministry of Road Transport and Highways guidelines. The same 35×45 mm, 300 DPI, 50 KB rule applies nationwide.
