Learner's & driving licence photo size (Sarathi)

Quick answer

For a learner's licence on Sarathi Parivahan, upload a JPEG between 10 KB and 20 KB; 420 × 525 pixels is a safe size. For the permanent driving licence, many RTOs take your photo at the driving test, so no upload is needed.

The Sarathi upload step is known for a strict and small size window: the error commonly reported by applicants is that the file must be between 10 KB and 20 KB. The preset targets that range at 420 × 525 pixels. If your state's page shows a different limit, create a custom preset with those numbers.

When you'll be asked for a photo

  • Learner's licence (LL) — usually upload photo and signature online, unless you complete Aadhaar e-KYC, in which case some states use the Aadhaar photo.
  • Driving licence (DL) — many RTOs take your photo and signature on a camera at the time of the driving test.
  • Renewal / duplicate — depends on the state; some ask for an upload, some reuse the photo on record.

Photo rules

  • Recent and in colour. Most portals want a photo taken in the last 6 months (UPSC and NEET are stricter — see their guides).
  • Plain, light background. White or off-white with no pattern, shadow or other people.
  • Face the camera. Neutral expression, eyes open, mouth closed, head straight — not tilted.
  • Face fills most of the frame. Roughly 70–80% of the photo height from chin to top of head.
  • No caps or dark glasses. Religious head coverings are fine if the full face is visible.
  • Even light. Face a window; avoid overhead bulbs that cast shadows under the eyes.

Fixing common upload errors

  • “File size should be between 10 KB and 20 KB” — use the preset; it raises or lowers compression to land inside the window.
  • Photo looks stretched — you resized without keeping the 4:5 shape. Crop first, then resize.
  • Upload button does nothing — the portal accepts .jpg/.jpeg only, not .png or .heic.

Frequently asked questions

What size photo is required for a learner's licence?

Sarathi expects a JPEG between 10 KB and 20 KB. Our preset outputs 420 × 525 pixels inside that range.

Do I need a photo for a permanent driving licence?

Often not — many RTOs capture it at the driving test. Check your appointment slip or state transport website.

What about the signature?

Sarathi also asks for a signature on the learner's licence form. Limits vary by state; check the on-screen size and use a custom preset. See the signature guide for scanning tips.

Always check the portal

Limits on government portals change between application cycles. If the form shows a different size, use those numbers — the converter's Create custom preset lets you enter any size.
