How to Take a Good Ration Card Photo at Home

You don't need a professional studio to take a ration-card-ready photo. With a smartphone and a few simple tricks, you can get a compliant result at home.

  • Use natural lighting. Stand facing a window during daytime. Avoid overhead ceiling lights — they create unflattering shadows.
  • Choose a plain white wall. A clean, texture-free wall works best. If you don't have one, hang a white bedsheet behind you.
  • Eliminate shadows. Make sure no shadow falls on your face or the background. Diffuse window light by pulling a thin curtain if the light is too harsh.
  • Ask someone else to click the photo. Selfies often introduce distortion and poor framing. Have a family member stand a few feet away and use the rear camera for better quality.
