The National Testing Agency (NTA) conducts major entrance exams including NEET (medical), JEE Main (engineering), GATE (postgraduate), and CUET. These exams require a specific passport-size photograph during registration that differs from other government form photo requirements.

NTA Photo Rules — Important Details

  • Photo must be recent — within the last 6 months
  • White background — no patterns, shadows, or coloured walls
  • Face should cover 70% of the frame
  • No caps or hats unless for religious reasons
  • Prescription glasses allowed but no glare, no tinted glasses
  • Both ears visible for biometric matching on exam day

How to Take a Good NTA Exam Photo at Home

Getting a compliant NEET, JEE, or GATE photo at home is easy with these practical tips. A proper photo prevents registration rejections and exam day issues.

  • Use a recent photo — your photo must be taken within the last 6 months. Do not use old photos from earlier classes.
  • Avoid uniforms — do not wear your school or college uniform. Plain casual or formal wear is recommended.
  • White background is mandatory — stand before a plain white wall. Avoid off-white, cream, or any coloured backgrounds.
  • Both ears must be visible — NTA uses ear-to-ear measurement for biometric matching on exam day. Pull your hair back completely.
  • Avoid sweaters with high collars — high neck collars, turtlenecks, and scarves can cover the neck area. Wear a simple collared or round-neck shirt.
  • Keep a neutral expression — no smiling, frowning, or raised eyebrows. Face the camera directly.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the NEET photo size in pixels?NEET requires a 413×531 px photo at 300 DPI, equivalent to 3.5 cm × 4.5 cm.
Is the JEE photo size same as NEET?Yes, both NEET and JEE follow the same NTA standards — 413×531 px, 300 DPI, white background, max 200 KB.
Can I wear glasses in my NEET/JEE photo?Yes, but ensure no glare on lenses. Tinted glasses are not allowed.
What is the postcard photo size for NEET?NEET also requires a postcard photo (4×6 inches, 1200×1800 px at 300 DPI). Our tool includes a dedicated NEET Postcard Photo preset.
Can I use a selfie for NTA exam registration?Selfies are not recommended as they often fail the face coverage and background requirements. Use a proper passport-style photo.
Can I use my 10th standard photo for NEET?No, your NEET photo must be taken within the last 6 months. Using an old 10th standard photo will likely be rejected as it will not match your current appearance for exam day verification.
What if I am wearing a hijab in my NEET photo?Religious head coverings are permitted, but your face must be fully visible from the bottom of the chin to the top of the forehead, and both ears must remain uncovered for biometric matching.
Is there a separate photo requirement for NEET PG?NEET PG follows the same NTA photo standards: 413×531 px at 300 DPI, white background, JPEG format under 200 KB. The requirements are identical to NEET UG.
