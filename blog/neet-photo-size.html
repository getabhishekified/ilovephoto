NEET UG photo, postcard photo and signature size

Quick answer

NEET UG needs a passport-size photo and a 4 × 6 inch postcard photo, each a JPEG between 10 KB and 200 KB, on a white background with the face covering about 80% of the frame. Since 2026 a live photo is also captured by webcam in the form. Use the same recent photo for both uploads.

NEET UG asks for three images of you: a live photo captured in the form, a passport-size photo and a 4 × 6 inch postcard photo. The two uploaded photos must be the same recent photo and must match how you look in the live capture.

The live photo — what changed

From the 2026 cycle, NTA added a mandatory live photo, taken through your webcam or phone (via a QR code) while filling the form, and matched against your Aadhaar. You can't upload a file for this step, so a converter can't help — just sit facing a window against a plain wall, remove glasses and caps, and look at the camera.

You still upload the passport-size and postcard photos below. Use the same photo session for both, so all three images clearly look like the same person on the same day.

Published NEET signature limits have differed between years (4–30 KB, 10–100 KB). The preset targets 10–30 KB, which satisfies both. Thumb and finger impressions are also required — scan them on white paper at good resolution and check the size window shown in the form.

Photo rules

  • Recent and in colour. Most portals want a photo taken in the last 6 months (UPSC and NEET are stricter — see their guides).
  • Plain, light background. White or off-white with no pattern, shadow or other people.
  • Face the camera. Neutral expression, eyes open, mouth closed, head straight — not tilted.
  • Face fills most of the frame. Roughly 70–80% of the photo height from chin to top of head.
  • No caps or dark glasses. Religious head coverings are fine if the full face is visible.
  • Even light. Face a window; avoid overhead bulbs that cast shadows under the eyes.

NEET bulletins in recent years have also required the photo to be taken in the current year — don't reuse your Class 10 or Class 12 photo.

Frequently asked questions

What is the NEET photo size in KB?

Both the passport-size and postcard photos must be JPEG files between 10 KB and 200 KB.

Is the postcard photo different from the passport photo?

It should be the same recent photo, just at 4 × 6 inches. Different photos are a common reason for discrepancy notices.

Can I upload a photo for the live capture step?

No. The live photo has to be taken through the camera during the application. Use good light and a plain background.

Does NEET require my name and date on the photo?

Check the current information bulletin. If it asks for it, the converter's “Name & date strip” option adds it for you.

Is JEE Main the same?

JEE Main 2026 also moved to live photo capture. Follow the instructions in the JEE Main bulletin; this converter only helps with photos you upload as files.

Always check the portal

Limits on government portals change between application cycles. If the form shows a different size, use those numbers — the converter's Create custom preset lets you enter any size.
