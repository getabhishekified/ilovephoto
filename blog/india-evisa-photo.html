How to Take a Good e-Visa Photo at Home

Taking a compliant India e-Visa photo at home is simple with the right setup. Follow these tips to avoid application delays:

  • Use a plain light wall — white or off-white walls work best. Avoid textured walls, patterns, or visible shadows.
  • Ensure even lighting — stand facing a window or use two light sources on either side to eliminate harsh shadows on your face.
  • Avoid jewellery — remove earrings, necklaces, nose rings, and facial piercings that may obscure your features.
  • Keep ears visible — pull long hair behind your ears so both ears are clearly visible in the frame.
  • No tinted glasses — remove sunglasses and tinted prescription eyewear. Clear lenses without glare are acceptable.
  • Look straight at the camera — maintain a neutral expression, mouth closed, with both eyes open and visible.
