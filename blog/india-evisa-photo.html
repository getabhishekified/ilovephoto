India e-Visa photo requirements

Quick answer

The Indian e-Visa photo must be a square JPEG (height equal to width) between 10 KB and 300 KB, showing the full face on a plain white background, centred, with no border.

Foreign nationals applying for an Indian e-Visa upload a square photo of their face. A poor photo is one of the most common reasons an e-Visa application is sent back.

The official instructions ask for a JPEG with equal height and width, between 10 KB and 300 KB. Some guides quote a 1 MB limit for e-Visa; staying under 300 KB satisfies both.

Photo rules

  • Recent and in colour. Most portals want a photo taken in the last 6 months (UPSC and NEET are stricter — see their guides).
  • Plain, light background. White or off-white with no pattern, shadow or other people.
  • Face the camera. Neutral expression, eyes open, mouth closed, head straight — not tilted.
  • Face fills most of the frame. Roughly 70–80% of the photo height from chin to top of head.
  • No caps or dark glasses. Religious head coverings are fine if the full face is visible.
  • Even light. Face a window; avoid overhead bulbs that cast shadows under the eyes.

The e-Visa photo should show the full face from the top of the hair to the chin, centred in the frame, with no border.

Passport page scan

The e-Visa also asks for a scan of your passport bio-data page as a PDF. That isn't a photo upload — scan it flat, in colour, and make sure the machine-readable lines at the bottom are sharp.

Frequently asked questions

What size photo for an Indian e-Visa?

Square JPEG, minimum 10 KB, maximum 300 KB. Our preset outputs 600 × 600 pixels.

Can I wear glasses?

Avoid them. If you must, make sure there is no glare and your eyes are clearly visible.

Always check the portal

Limits on government portals change between application cycles. If the form shows a different size, use those numbers — the converter's Create custom preset lets you enter any size.
