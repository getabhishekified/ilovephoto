A rejected passport photo is one of the most common reasons for passport application delays in India. The Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) has strict guidelines on photo dimensions, background, face coverage, and file specifications that every applicant must follow.

Indian Passport Photo Rules — What MEA Requires

The MEA follows ICAO (International Civil Aviation Organization) standards for passport photos. These international standards ensure that passport photos are consistent across all countries and can be used for biometric verification at airports.

Photo Composition Rules

RequirementSpecification

BackgroundPlain white or off-white, no shadows or patterns
Eye height from bottomApproximately 60% of photo height
LightingEven, no shadows on face or background

Glasses and Accessories Rules

The MEA has specific rules regarding what you can wear in your passport photo:

  • Glasses: It is highly recommended to remove glasses. If you must wear them, ensure there is no glare on the lenses, the frames do not cover your eyes, and they are not tinted.
  • Head coverings: Allowed only for religious reasons. The face must be fully visible from forehead to chin with no shadows cast by the covering.
  • Hair: Should not cover the face or eyes. If you have long hair, keep it behind your ears.
  • Uniforms: Do not wear military, police, or other official uniforms. Regular civilian clothes are required.
  • Jewelry: Minimal and not obscuring any facial features.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the exact passport photo size in mm for India? The exact size is 35 mm wide × 45 mm tall at 300 DPI. This is equivalent to 630 × 810 pixels.
Is a white background mandatory for Indian passports? Yes, a plain white or off-white background is required. Avoid coloured walls, patterns, or gradients.
Can I wear glasses in my passport photo? It is highly recommended to remove them. If you must wear glasses, ensure no glare and that your eyes are clearly visible.
What is the ideal face coverage for a passport photo? Your face should occupy 70% to 80% of the photo frame. The distance from the bottom of your chin to the top of your head should be about 32 mm.
What file size is required for passport photo upload? The maximum file size is 100 KB at 300 DPI in JPEG format.
Can I use my passport photo for other documents? No. Each government form has unique requirements. PAN card needs 200×276 px at 200 DPI, while Voter ID requires 35×45 mm in colour. Always use the correct preset for each document.
