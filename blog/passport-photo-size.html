Frequently Asked Questions

Is a white background mandatory for Indian passports? Yes, a plain, light-colored (preferably white) background is required to avoid shadows and distractions.
Can I wear glasses in my passport photo? It is highly recommended to remove glasses to avoid glare on the lenses, which can lead to photo rejection.
What is the ideal face ratio for the photo? Your face should occupy about 70% to 80% of the photo area, with a clear view of your eyes and chin.
