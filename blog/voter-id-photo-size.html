Voter ID photo size for Form 6 and Form 8

Quick answer

The Voter ID (EPIC) photo for Form 6 or Form 8 is a recent passport-size colour photo, 3.5 × 4.5 cm, on a plain white background, uploaded as JPEG. At 300 DPI that is 413 × 531 pixels; keeping it under 100 KB works with every published limit.

When you register as a new voter (Form 6) or correct your details (Form 8) online, you upload your own passport-size photo. It is printed on your EPIC card, so a clear photo matters.

The ECI asks for a recent passport-size colour photo (3.5 × 4.5 cm) with a plain white background showing your full face. Published file limits vary between guides, so the preset keeps the file under 100 KB — small enough for every limit we've seen, while still sharp enough to print.

Where you upload it

  • Form 6 — new registration, including first-time voters turning 18.
  • Form 8 — correction of entries, including replacing the photo, or shifting your address.
  • Both are available on voters.eci.gov.in and the Voter Helpline app.

If you apply offline at your Booth Level Officer, you attach a printed passport-size photo instead — use the converter's Print sheet to get several on one A4 page.

Photo rules

  • Recent and in colour. Most portals want a photo taken in the last 6 months (UPSC and NEET are stricter — see their guides).
  • Plain, light background. White or off-white with no pattern, shadow or other people.
  • Face the camera. Neutral expression, eyes open, mouth closed, head straight — not tilted.
  • Face fills most of the frame. Roughly 70–80% of the photo height from chin to top of head.
  • No caps or dark glasses. Religious head coverings are fine if the full face is visible.
  • Even light. Face a window; avoid overhead bulbs that cast shadows under the eyes.

Getting the photo approved

  • Use the same look you'll have at the polling booth — the photo is used to identify you.
  • Don't upload a photo of your old voter card or another ID — it will be blurry and is often rejected.
  • Check the preview before submitting: the app doesn't let you replace the photo after submission without filing Form 8.

Frequently asked questions

What is the voter ID photo size?

A passport-size colour photo, 3.5 × 4.5 cm, on a white background. Our preset outputs 413 × 531 pixels at 300 DPI, under 100 KB.

How do I change the photo on my voter ID?

File Form 8 on voters.eci.gov.in or the Voter Helpline app, choose “Correction of entries”, select photograph and upload the new photo.

Can I use a selfie?

Only if it meets the rules: front-facing, plain light background, even light and no filters. Hold the phone at eye level, or ask someone to take it from about 1.5 m away.

Always check the portal

Limits on government portals change between application cycles. If the form shows a different size, use those numbers — the converter's Create custom preset lets you enter any size.
