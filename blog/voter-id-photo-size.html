Voter ID Photo Size & Requirements — Complete Guide (2026)

The Voter ID card (also called EPIC — Electors Photo Identity Card) is issued by the Election Commission of India. When applying for a new voter ID or updating your existing card, one of the most important requirements is uploading a correctly sized photo.

The photo on your voter ID must be a colour photograph with specific dimensions, DPI, and format. In this guide, we explain everything you need to know about Voter ID photo size requirements and how to get it right.

Voter ID Photo Requirements — Key Points

Unlike passport photos that need a strictly white background, Voter ID photos can have a light or off-white background. The key requirement is that the photo is in colour and clearly shows your face.

  • Colour photo is mandatory — black and white photos are not accepted
  • Plain background — avoid patterned walls or busy backgrounds
  • Face clearly visible — no obstructions, both ears visible if possible
  • No glare or reflections — especially important if wearing glasses
  • Even lighting — no harsh shadows on your face

How to Take a Good Voter ID Photo at Home

Taking a voter ID photo at home can save you time and money. Here are some additional tips to ensure your photo meets Election Commission standards:

  • Natural daylight is best — take your photo near a window during the daytime for soft, even lighting. Avoid direct sunlight which creates harsh shadows.
  • Avoid patterned clothing — stripes, checks, and fine patterns can cause moire effects on the printed EPIC card. Solid colours work best.
  • Stand at least 2 feet away from the wall — this prevents shadows from appearing on the background and gives a cleaner look.
  • Take multiple shots and pick the best — even small differences in head angle or expression can make a big difference. Shoot 5-10 photos and select the sharpest one.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Voter ID photo size in mm?The Voter ID photo size is 35 mm × 45 mm (width × height). At 300 DPI this equals 413 × 531 pixels.
Do I need a colour photo for Voter ID?Yes, a colour photograph is mandatory for Voter ID applications. Black and white photos will be rejected.
What background colour is required for Voter ID photo?A plain white or light-coloured background is recommended. Avoid dark colours, patterns, or outdoor backgrounds.
Can I use my passport photo for Voter ID?The dimensions are the same (35×45 mm) but the DPI and file size limits differ. Use the correct preset for each document.
What is the maximum file size for Voter ID photo?The Election Commission portal accepts photos up to 2 MB in size.
Can I use my voter ID photo for passport application?No, you cannot use the same photo. While both are 35×45 mm, passport photos require a strictly white background and a smaller file size (100 KB max), whereas voter ID photos allow a light background and up to 2 MB file size.
What if my EPIC card photo doesn't look like me?You can request a photo correction by submitting Form EPIC-001 at your nearest Election Commission office or through the NVSP portal. A recent colour photograph with the correct specifications is required.
Do I need to upload a photo for voter ID correction?Yes, any correction or update to your voter ID (including name, address, or photo) on the NVSP portal requires uploading a fresh colour photograph meeting the 35×45 mm, 300 DPI specifications.
