How to Take a Good LinkedIn Photo at Home

A professional LinkedIn photo doesn't require a studio. With the right setup, you can take a recruiter-ready headshot using just your smartphone.

  • Dress one level above your industry. For corporate fields, a blazer or collared shirt works. For creative roles, smart casual is fine — just avoid logos and busy patterns.
  • Use natural window light. Position yourself facing a window so the light falls evenly on your face. Avoid mixing window light with room light.
  • Smile with teeth vs. without. A closed-mouth smile conveys confidence and warmth. A teeth-showing smile works if it looks natural — practice in a mirror first.
  • Choose a clean background. A plain wall, a bookshelf, or a blurred office background all work well. Avoid bathrooms, cluttered rooms, or outdoor scenery.
  • What to wear. Solid colours photograph best. Navy, grey, and pastels are safe choices. Avoid all-white (washes out) and busy stripes or checks.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

MistakeHow to Fix
Using selfiesUse a tripod or ask someone to take the photo. Selfies distort facial proportions.
Busy or distracting backgroundsStand in front of a plain wall, or use the background blur feature on your phone.
Filters and effectsAvoid Instagram filters, beauty modes, or heavy editing — they look unprofessional.
Cropped group photosNever crop someone else out of a group photo. Take a fresh solo headshot instead.
Outdated photos (more than 2 years old)Your profile photo should reflect your current appearance. Refresh it every 1–2 years.

What background should I use for LinkedIn photo?A plain, professional background works best. White, light grey, or a professional office background are all good choices.
Should I smile in my LinkedIn photo?Yes, a natural, approachable smile is recommended. Profiles with smiling photos receive more connection requests.
What file format is best for LinkedIn photo?JPEG is recommended for smaller file sizes. PNG is also acceptable if you need transparency or higher quality.

How often should I update my LinkedIn photo?Every 1–2 years or whenever your appearance changes significantly (new hairstyle, glasses, weight change). An up-to-date photo builds trust with recruiters and connections.
