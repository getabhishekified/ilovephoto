LinkedIn profile photo size (and résumé photos)

Quick answer

LinkedIn profile photos should be at least 400 × 400 pixels; 800 × 800 pixels stays sharp everywhere. Files up to 8 MB are accepted. Keep your face centred because the photo is shown as a circle.

LinkedIn accepts large photos, but displays them as a small circle. A square 800 × 800 pixel image, framed tight on your face, stays sharp everywhere it appears.

LinkedIn recommends at least 400 × 400 pixels; larger is fine. Because the photo is shown as a circle, keep your face in the centre and leave some space around your head.

What makes a good profile photo

  • Your face takes up about 60% of the frame.
  • Soft daylight from the front; avoid harsh overhead light.
  • Plain or softly blurred background.
  • Dress as you would for work in your field.
  • Smile naturally — unlike ID photos, a friendly expression helps here.

Résumé photos

Many Indian employers and government job forms still expect a passport-size photo on the CV. Use a 3.5 × 4.5 cm image (the Voter ID preset produces one) and place it at the top-right.

Frequently asked questions

What is the LinkedIn profile photo size?

Minimum 400 × 400 pixels. 800 × 800 pixels is a good choice.

Should I use my passport photo on LinkedIn?

You can, but a warmer, well-lit headshot with a natural smile usually works better.

Always check the portal

Limits on government portals change between application cycles. If the form shows a different size, use those numbers — the converter's Create custom preset lets you enter any size.
