Schengen visa photo size (35 × 45 mm)

Most Schengen countries ask you to bring printed 35 × 45 mm photos to your VFS or embassy appointment. Because you print them yourself, you control the size — which is where this converter helps.

Schengen photos follow the ICAO standard used for European passports: 35 mm wide × 45 mm high, with the face (chin to crown) taking 70–80% of the height — about 32–36 mm.

Printing at home or at a shop

  1. Pick the Schengen preset and frame your face using the face guide.
  2. Click Print sheet. The converter fills an A4 page with photos at the exact 35 × 45 mm size.
  3. In the print dialog, set scale to 100% / actual size (not “fit to page”).
  4. Print on photo paper, or take the downloaded file to a print shop and ask for actual-size printing.
  5. Measure one photo with a ruler — it should be exactly 35 mm wide.

Photo rules

  • Recent and in colour. Most portals want a photo taken in the last 6 months (UPSC and NEET are stricter — see their guides).
  • Plain, light background. White or off-white with no pattern, shadow or other people.
  • Face the camera. Neutral expression, eyes open, mouth closed, head straight — not tilted.
  • Face fills most of the frame. Roughly 70–80% of the photo height from chin to top of head.
  • No caps or dark glasses. Religious head coverings are fine if the full face is visible.
  • Even light. Face a window; avoid overhead bulbs that cast shadows under the eyes.

Some countries (for example Germany and France) are strict about head size and background. Check the checklist from the embassy or VFS centre for your destination.

Frequently asked questions

How many photos do I need for a Schengen visa?

Usually one or two, depending on the country. Check your embassy or VFS checklist.

Is a 2 × 2 inch photo acceptable?

No. Schengen photos must be 35 × 45 mm. 2 × 2 inch is the US size.

Always check the portal

Limits on government portals change between application cycles. If the form shows a different size, use those numbers — the converter's Create custom preset lets you enter any size.
