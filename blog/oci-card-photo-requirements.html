OCI card photo and signature requirements

The OCI application on ociservices.gov.in asks for a square photo and a wide 3:1 signature, uploaded as JPEG files. You also give printed photos to the embassy or VFS centre in some countries.

The signature must have a 3:1 width-to-height ratio. Minors who can't sign can use a left thumb impression; see the OCI portal for the exact rule.

Photo rules

  • Recent and in colour. Most portals want a photo taken in the last 6 months (UPSC and NEET are stricter — see their guides).
  • Plain, light background. White or off-white with no pattern, shadow or other people.
  • Face the camera. Neutral expression, eyes open, mouth closed, head straight — not tilted.
  • Face fills most of the frame. Roughly 70–80% of the photo height from chin to top of head.
  • No caps or dark glasses. Religious head coverings are fine if the full face is visible.
  • Even light. Face a window; avoid overhead bulbs that cast shadows under the eyes.

The OCI portal is strict about the face being centred with both ears visible and no shadow on the background.

Frequently asked questions

What is the OCI photo size in pixels?

Square, between 200 × 200 and roughly 900–1500 pixels. Our preset outputs 600 × 600 pixels under 200 KB.

Why does the OCI portal reject my signature?

Usually the ratio. It must be 3:1 (for example 600 × 200 px). Crop tight around the signature first.

Always check the portal

Limits on government portals change between application cycles. If the form shows a different size, use those numbers — the converter's Create custom preset lets you enter any size.
