How to Take a Good UPSC Photo at Home

Preparing a UPSC-compliant photo at home is straightforward. Follow these tips to get your application photo right the first time:

  • Dress code: Wear formal civilian attire — a collared shirt, kurta, or blazer works well. Avoid uniforms, casual T-shirts, or striped patterns that may cause moiré effects.
  • Expression: Keep a neutral, natural expression with both eyes open and mouth closed. Do not smile with teeth showing or frown. A calm, composed look is ideal for the UPSC interview stage as well.
  • No uniforms: Military, police, or any government service uniforms are strictly prohibited. Even if you are currently serving, wear plain civilian clothes only.
  • Square framing: The UPSC photo must be perfectly square (1:1 ratio). Position yourself centrally with adequate headroom — your face should occupy roughly 70% of the frame.
  • Lighting: Use soft, natural daylight from a window. Avoid direct flash that causes red-eye or harsh shadows. The light should fall evenly on your face without creating shadows on the white background.
