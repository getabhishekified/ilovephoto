UPSC photo and signature size

UPSC uses the same photo rules across its exams (Civil Services, ESE, CDS, NDA, CAPF and others). You upload the photo once to your One-Time Registration (OTR) profile and reuse it.

UPSC instructions in recent years have given a photo size of 20–300 KB and dimensions between 350 and 1000 pixels per side. Some 2026 guidance quotes a lower 200 KB cap, so the preset stays at 20–200 KB to be safe either way.

Name and date on the photo

UPSC has asked candidates to submit a photo that is recent (taken shortly before the application window opens) and that shows the candidate's name and the date the photo was taken printed below the face. Check whether the current notice still asks for this.

The converter has a Name & date strip option. Turn it on, type your name as in your application, and pick the date the photo was taken. The strip is added below the face without squashing it.

Photo rules

  • Recent and in colour. Most portals want a photo taken in the last 6 months (UPSC and NEET are stricter — see their guides).
  • Plain, light background. White or off-white with no pattern, shadow or other people.
  • Face the camera. Neutral expression, eyes open, mouth closed, head straight — not tilted.
  • Face fills most of the frame. Roughly 70–80% of the photo height from chin to top of head.
  • No caps or dark glasses. Religious head coverings are fine if the full face is visible.
  • Even light. Face a window; avoid overhead bulbs that cast shadows under the eyes.

Keep the photo consistent with the one you'll carry: UPSC may ask for printed copies of the same photo at the interview or exam.

Frequently asked questions

What is the UPSC photo size?

A JPEG between 20 KB and 200–300 KB depending on the notice, with each side between 350 and 1000 pixels. Our preset outputs 600 × 750 pixels, 20–200 KB.

How old can my UPSC photo be?

UPSC has required the photo to be taken very recently — within about 10 days before the application window opened in recent cycles. Take a fresh photo for each cycle.

How do I add my name and date to the photo?

Pick the UPSC preset, switch on “Name & date strip”, fill in your name and the date, then download.

Always check the portal

Limits on government portals change between application cycles. If the form shows a different size, use those numbers — the converter's Create custom preset lets you enter any size.
