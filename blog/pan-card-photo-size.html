PAN card photo and signature size (Protean & UTIITSL)

Quick answer

For a Protean (NSDL) PAN application, the photo is 2.5 × 3.5 cm at 200 DPI — 197 × 276 pixels, JPEG, under 50 KB. For UTIITSL it is a square 213 × 213 pixels, under about 30 KB. The Protean signature is 4.5 × 2 cm at 200 DPI (354 × 157 px), under 50 KB. No upload is needed if you apply with Aadhaar e-KYC.

You only upload a PAN photo when you apply through Protean or UTIITSL and choose to submit scanned documents. If you apply with Aadhaar e-KYC, your photo comes straight from Aadhaar and there's nothing to upload.

Photo rules

  • Recent and in colour. Most portals want a photo taken in the last 6 months (UPSC and NEET are stricter — see their guides).
  • Plain, light background. White or off-white with no pattern, shadow or other people.
  • Face the camera. Neutral expression, eyes open, mouth closed, head straight — not tilted.
  • Face fills most of the frame. Roughly 70–80% of the photo height from chin to top of head.
  • No caps or dark glasses. Religious head coverings are fine if the full face is visible.
  • Even light. Face a window; avoid overhead bulbs that cast shadows under the eyes.

Why PAN photos get rejected

  • Photo and signature swapped — the most common slip. Check each upload box before submitting.
  • Old or unclear photo — a blurry phone shot or a photo of a printed photo.
  • Signature on lined paper or with the date written beside it.
  • File over the limit — the portal silently refuses files above 50 KB (Protean) or 30 KB (UTIITSL).

Frequently asked questions

Is the PAN card photo size 2.5 × 3.5 cm or 3.5 × 4.5 cm?

For PAN it is 2.5 cm wide × 3.5 cm high. 3.5 × 4.5 cm is the general passport-size used by many other forms.

What is the PAN card photo size in pixels?

For Protean (NSDL) it is 197 × 276 pixels at 200 DPI, under 50 KB. For UTIITSL it is a square 213 × 213 pixels at 300 DPI.

Can I change my PAN card photo online?

Yes. Apply for “Changes or Correction in PAN data” on Protean or UTIITSL and tick the photo mismatch option. Choose the scanned-images route so you can upload a new photo.

Do I need a photo for instant e-PAN?

No. Instant e-PAN uses your Aadhaar details, including the Aadhaar photo.

Always check the portal

Limits on government portals change between application cycles. If the form shows a different size, use those numbers — the converter's Create custom preset lets you enter any size.
