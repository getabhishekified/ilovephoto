UK Passport Photo Size & Requirements — Complete Guide (2026)

UK passport photo requirements differ from Indian passport photos in several important ways. The most notable difference is the background colour — UK passports require a cream or light-grey background rather than pure white. HM Passport Office (HMPO) has strict guidelines that all applicants must follow.

Whether you are a British citizen applying for a first passport or an Indian national applying for a UK visa, this guide covers everything you need to know.

UK Passport Photo Rules

UK passport photos must follow these rules:

  • Cream or light grey background — not pure white like Indian passports
  • Photo must show a clear front view of the full face
  • Eyes must be open and clearly visible (no hair covering eyes)
  • Neutral expression with mouth closed
  • No shadows on the face or background
  • No glasses except in exceptional circumstances with medical proof
  • No headwear unless for religious or medical reasons
  • Photo must be less than one month old

How to Take a Good UK Passport Photo at Home

Taking a UK passport photo at home is straightforward if you follow HMPO guidelines. Unlike Indian passport photos that use a pure white background, UK passport photos require a cream or light-grey background. Here are some tips to get it right:

  • Use even, diffused lighting — position yourself facing a window or use two light sources from either side to avoid shadows on your face or background.
  • Choose the right background — a cream or light-grey wall works well. Avoid pure white walls as HMPO specifically requires a cream/light-grey tone.
  • No red-eye correction — do not use red-eye reduction filters or beauty mode on your phone camera as these alter facial features and may cause rejection.
  • Frame carefully — keep your head centred with your face taking up about 60–70% of the frame. Your chin to crown should be well within the photo area.
  • Take multiple shots — digital photos are free, so take several and pick the best one that meets all requirements.
