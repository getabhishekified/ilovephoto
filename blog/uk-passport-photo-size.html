UK passport photo size (online application)

Quick answer

For an online UK passport application, the photo must be at least 600 pixels wide and 750 pixels tall, between 50 KB and 10 MB, in colour, against a plain light background, and taken in the last month.

British citizens applying or renewing online upload a digital photo, which you can take yourself. (UK visa applicants have their photo taken at the visa application centre, so they don't need this.)

Rules specific to UK photos

  • Plain, light-coloured background that contrasts with you — cream and light grey work well.
  • Include head, shoulders and upper body; don't crop too tight — the online checker crops for you.
  • Plain expression, mouth closed, eyes open, no hair across the eyes.
  • Avoid glasses unless necessary; never sunglasses or tinted lenses.
  • Not edited with software (no filters or retouching).

Frequently asked questions

Can I take my own UK passport photo?

Yes, for online applications. Ask someone else to take it — selfies are not accepted.

Is a UK visa photo the same?

UK visa applicants usually have their photo taken at the visa centre during biometrics, so no upload is needed.

Always check the portal

Limits on government portals change between application cycles. If the form shows a different size, use those numbers — the converter's Create custom preset lets you enter any size.
