Aadhar Photo Guidelines — What You Should Know

Unlike PAN card or passport applications where you upload a photo yourself, Aadhar enrollment photos are typically taken at the enrollment centre. However, if you are updating your photo online or applying through certain channels, these guidelines apply:

  • Recent photo required — your photo should be no more than 6 months old
  • Plain white background — no patterns, shadows, or gradients
  • Passport-size format — similar to passport photo dimensions
  • No obstructions — keep hair away from face, avoid large jewellery
  • Neutral expression — both eyes open, mouth closed, facing forward

How to Take a Good Aadhar Photo at Home

While UIDAI typically captures your photo at the enrollment centre, you may need to provide a digital photo for online Aadhar updates or corrections. Follow these tips to get a clean, compliant photo at home:

  • Use proper lighting — sit facing a window during daytime for soft, natural light. Avoid overhead lights that cast harsh shadows on your face.
  • Keep your face fully visible — ensure no hair covers your eyes, eyebrows, or ears. Remove large jewellery or accessories that may obstruct facial features.
  • Frame at ear level — position the camera at eye level, about arm's length away. Your face should be centred and take up most of the frame.
  • Avoid tilting your head — keep your head straight and facing the camera directly. A slight tilt can cause the photo to be rejected during biometric matching.
  • Stand against a plain white wall — ensure the background is clean, untextured, and evenly lit without shadows.

Frequently Asked Questions

What photo size is required for Aadhar card?Aadhar card requires a standard passport-size photo (35×45 mm or 413×531 px at 300 DPI) with a white background.
Can I update my Aadhar photo online?Yes, you can update your Aadhar photo through the UIDAI portal. The photo must meet the specified requirements to be accepted.
Is a white background necessary for Aadhar photo?Yes, a plain white background is strongly recommended. Avoid coloured walls, curtains, or outdoor settings.
What is the DPI requirement for Aadhar photo?300 DPI is recommended for clear print quality on the Aadhar card.
What is the Aadhar photo size for children?The same passport-size photo (35×45 mm or 413×531 px at 300 DPI) is used for children's Aadhar cards. For children below 5 years, a smaller face-to-frame ratio may be accepted. The photo should be taken against a white background with the child's face clearly visible.
Do I need a new photo for Aadhar PVC card update?If you are simply updating your standard Aadhar card to the new PVC card format without changing any details, you do not need a new photo. The existing photo on your Aadhar record will be printed on the PVC card.
