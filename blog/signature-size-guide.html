Signature size for online application forms

Almost every form that asks for a photo also asks for your signature. Signatures are where most upload errors happen: wrong shape, too small a file, or a grey shadowy background.

How to capture a clean signature

  1. Sign in the middle of a plain white A4 sheet with a black ball-point or gel pen. Sign at your normal size.
  2. Photograph it in daylight, holding the phone directly above the paper so the signature isn't slanted.
  3. In the converter, pick your signature preset, then use Crop to cut tight around the signature.
  4. Raise Contrast (about 150–180%) and Brightness a little until the paper is pure white and the ink is dark.
  5. Download and check the file: the signature should fill most of the box and be easy to read.

Common signature mistakes

  • Lots of empty paper around the signature — the signature becomes tiny after resizing. Crop tight.
  • Grey background — shadows from your phone. Increase brightness and contrast.
  • Signature on lined or coloured paper — use plain white.
  • Different from your usual signature — it may be compared with your signature at the exam or bank.

Frequently asked questions

Should my signature be in black or blue ink?

Black is safest — it scans with the most contrast. Most forms accept either.

How do I reduce my signature file to under 20 KB?

Pick a signature preset — it compresses to fit the limit automatically. If a form has a minimum size, the converter also makes sure the file isn't too small.

Always check the portal

Limits on government portals change between application cycles. If the form shows a different size, use those numbers — the converter's Create custom preset lets you enter any size.
