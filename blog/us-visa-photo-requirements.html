US visa photo requirements (DS-160)

Every US visa applicant uploads a digital photo in the DS-160 form. The same rules apply to US passport photos. The rules are strict, and the photo is checked again at the consulate.

The photo must be square, between 600 × 600 and 1200 × 1200 pixels, in colour, JPEG, and 240 KB or smaller. The head (chin to top of hair) should take up 50–69% of the image height.

Rules specific to US photos

  • No glasses — not even clear prescription glasses, unless you have a medical statement.
  • Taken in the last 6 months and showing your current appearance.
  • White or off-white background with no shadows.
  • No filters or edits — don't smooth skin or change colours.
  • Head coverings only for religious or medical reasons, with the full face visible.

Framing tip

Turn on the face guide in the converter. Zoom so the oval runs from your chin to just above your hair, then drag to centre your face. Leave a little space above the head.

Frequently asked questions

What size is the US visa photo?

2 × 2 inches when printed. For the DS-160 upload: square JPEG, 600–1200 px per side, up to 240 KB.

Can I wear glasses in a US visa photo?

No. Glasses have not been allowed in US visa photos since 2016, except with a medical statement.

What if the DS-160 photo tool rejects my photo?

You can continue and bring a printed 2 × 2 inch photo to your visa appointment, but it is better to fix the upload: check the head size and background first.

Always check the portal

Limits on government portals change between application cycles. If the form shows a different size, use those numbers — the converter's Create custom preset lets you enter any size.
