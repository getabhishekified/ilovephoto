Photo and signature size chart for Indian forms (2026)

Quick answer

Indian passport-size photos are 3.5 × 4.5 cm (413 × 531 px at 300 DPI). Online forms differ mainly in file size: PAN under 50 KB, Sarathi 10–20 KB, IBPS 20–50 KB, NEET 10–200 KB, UPSC 20–200 KB, US visa under 240 KB. The full chart is below.

Frequently asked questions

What is passport size photo size in India?

3.5 × 4.5 cm. At 300 DPI that is 413 × 531 pixels; at 200 DPI it is 276 × 354 pixels.

What is the PAN card photo size?

2.5 × 3.5 cm at 200 DPI (197 × 276 pixels), JPEG under 50 KB for Protean; 213 × 213 pixels under 30 KB for UTIITSL.

Which forms don't need a photo upload?

Aadhaar and the Indian passport (photo taken at the centre), and JEE Main and SSC (live webcam capture).
