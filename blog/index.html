Government Form Photo Guides

Find the exact photo size, DPI, format, and background requirements for every Indian government document, exam, and visa application.

Applying for a government document in India often requires submitting a photograph that meets very specific technical specifications — precise pixel dimensions, exact DPI, strict file size limits, and particular background colours. Each agency sets its own rules: the Income Tax Department for PAN cards, the Election Commission for Voter ID, the Ministry of External Affairs for passports, and the Union Public Service Commission for civil services exams.

An incorrectly sized photo is one of the most common reasons for application rejection in India. A rejected application means losing your processing fee, waiting another 15–30 days, and potentially missing critical deadlines for exams or travel. These guides explain exactly what each form requires so you can get it right the first time.
