About ilovephoto.in

A free tool that turns any photo or signature into a file an Indian application form will accept — without uploading it anywhere.

Why we built it

Online forms reject photos for reasons that are hard to see: a file 3 KB over the limit, the wrong shape, or a size in centimetres that doesn't say how many pixels. Most “photo resizer” sites solve this by uploading your face to their server. We think a photo of your face and signature shouldn't leave your device to be resized.

How it works

The converter runs entirely in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. When you choose a document, it crops your photo to the right shape, scales it to the exact pixel size, writes the DPI into the file, and finds the highest JPEG quality that fits the KB limit. Nothing is sent to a server.

What we cover — and what we don't

We only include documents where you provide the photo: PAN, Voter ID, driving licence, exam forms like NEET, UPSC, IBPS and CAT, and visa and OCI applications. We don't include Aadhaar or the Indian passport, because those photos are taken at the centre, or exams like JEE Main and SSC that now use live camera capture. More on that here.

How we check specifications

Each guide lists what the portal asks for next to what our preset produces, and links to the official source. Portals don't always publish complete numbers, and different sources sometimes disagree; when that happens we choose values that satisfy every published limit and say so on the page. Specs were last reviewed in November 2026.

Independence

ilovephoto.in is an independent project. It isn't affiliated with any government department, exam body or embassy. The site is free to use and supported by ads.
