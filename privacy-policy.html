Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Introduction

ilovephoto.in ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our website handles your data when you use our photo conversion service.

No Data Collection

ilovephoto.in is designed to be completely private. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or images. All photo processing happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript and the HTML5 Canvas API. Your photos never leave your device.

No Server Uploads

Unlike many online photo converters, we do not upload your photos to any server. The entire conversion process — including image loading, resizing, cropping, format conversion, and compression — happens locally in your browser's memory. Once you close the page or refresh, your photo data is permanently cleared.

Local Storage

We use your browser's localStorage to save any custom presets you create. This data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand anonymous usage patterns (e.g., how many visitors use the site, which pages are visited). Google Analytics collects anonymized data such as:

  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on the site
  • Browser type and version
  • Approximate geographic location (city level only)

This data cannot be used to identify individual users. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Google AdSense

We may display advertisements through Google AdSense. Google uses cookies to serve personalized ads based on your browsing history. You can manage your ad personalization preferences at Google Ad Settings.

Contact Form

When you use our contact form, the message is sent via your email client (mailto: link). We do not store or process contact form submissions on any server.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — for typography. Google may collect font request data per its privacy policy.
  • Google Analytics — for anonymized usage analytics (as described above).
  • Google AdSense — for advertising (as described above).

Your Rights

As a user, you have the right to:

  • Use the site without providing any personal information
  • Use your browser's incognito/private mode to prevent localStorage persistence
  • Clear your browser data (cache, localStorage, cookies) at any time
  • Opt out of Google Analytics and personalized advertising

Children's Privacy

Our service is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us using the form on our homepage or via email at ilovephoto.in@gmail.com.
