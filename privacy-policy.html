Privacy policy

Last updated November 2026

Your photos stay on your device

Photos and signatures you open in the converter are processed in your browser's memory using JavaScript and the HTML5 Canvas API. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else. When you close or reload the page, they are gone. Camera access, if you use it, is only used to take a photo on your device and is switched off as soon as you capture or cancel.

What is stored in your browser

Custom presets you create are saved in your browser's local storage so they're there next time. You can delete them in the converter or by clearing site data.

Analytics and advertising

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used (for example pages viewed, browser type and approximate city-level location). We use Google AdSense to show ads; Google may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. Neither service receives your photos.

  • Manage ad personalisation at Google Ad Settings.
  • Opt out of Google Analytics with the browser add-on.
  • See how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Other third parties

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives a standard web request from your browser.

Contact form

The contact form opens your own email app with the message filled in. We receive it only if you send it, and we use your email address only to reply.

Children

The site is for a general audience. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
